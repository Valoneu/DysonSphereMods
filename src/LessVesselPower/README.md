# LessVesselPower
**Logistics Energy Optimization**

---

![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/Category-Energy-blue?style=flat-square)
![Efficiency](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-Efficiency-green?style=flat-square)

Reduces the energy requirements for logistics vessel trips to prevent power grid destabilization during large-scale exports.

---

## Features
* **Energy Reduction:** Multiplies the trip energy cost by a configurable factor.
* **Default Setup:** Preset to **25%** of vanilla energy cost for immediate power savings.
